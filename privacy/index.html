Deficify Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

Overview

Deficify is an educational health information app that helps people explore nutrient patterns related to everyday symptoms. It works without an account, advertising, analytics, or cloud storage.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. Deficify does not ask for your name, email address, location, identifiers, or account registration. Symptom selections, safety flags, supplement schedules, and app preferences stay on your device.

Data Storage

Deficify stores app data locally on your device. That local data is not transmitted to Deficify servers. If you delete the app, the locally stored app data is removed by your device operating system.

Purchases

Deficify is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes App Store purchases and payment information. Deficify does not receive or store your credit card details.

Third-Party Services

Deficify does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services in the app. The app includes educational links to outside sources such as NIH and Examine, which may be governed by their own privacy policies.

Health Information Disclaimer

Deficify is for educational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace clinician judgment. Persistent, severe, or worsening symptoms warrant a conversation with a qualified healthcare professional.

Children's Privacy

Deficify is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
