Deficify Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Overview

Deficify is an educational food scanner that helps people review packaged-food label data, nutrient wins, watchouts, and source notes. It works without an account, advertising, or selling personal data.

Information We Collect

Deficify does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, location, advertising identifier, or account registration inside the app. Saved scans, product snapshots, and app preferences stay on your device unless you choose to share or export information yourself.

Camera access is used only to scan UPC and EAN barcodes. You can enter barcode digits manually without granting camera access.

If you contact support by email, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send so we can respond to your request.

Data Storage

Deficify stores saved scans, product snapshots, scan quota state when enabled, entitlement state when enabled, and app preferences locally on your device. Saved scans remain on your device until you delete them, delete the app, or your device operating system removes the app data.

Development and field-test logs are disabled in production app use. If a debug or field-test export is used during testing, sharing that export is user-initiated.

Purchases

Deficify is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple processes App Store purchases and payment information. Deficify Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription purchased through Apple in-app purchase.

RevenueCat may process Apple purchase receipts, subscription status, entitlement status, and related technical identifiers so Deficify can unlock paid features. Deficify does not receive or store your credit card details.

Third-Party Services

Deficify does not use third-party advertising or tracking services in the app.

Barcode lookups may send the barcode number you scan or enter to Open Food Facts to retrieve available product label data. Open Food Facts may process that request under its own privacy practices. Open Food Facts data is available under ODbL and product photos under CC BY-SA.

The app may include educational links to outside sources. Opening those links may be governed by the destination website's own privacy policy.

Educational Label Review

Deficify is for educational label review only. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, prevent disease, provide medical advice, or replace clinician judgment.

Food databases can be incomplete or out of date. The package label is the source of truth. Always check the package label and talk with a qualified professional for medical, allergy, or nutrition decisions.

Terms and EULA

Unless Deficify posts a separate custom end user license agreement, use of the app is governed by Apple's Standard EULA for App Store apps and Apple's App Store terms. Deficify Plus subscriptions are billed through Apple and can be managed or canceled in your App Store account settings.

Children's Privacy

Deficify is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
