How Deficify works

Deficify is built for quick label review in the grocery aisle, pantry, or kitchen. It focuses on what the product label can support, not a universal health score.

  1. Scan or enter a barcode from a packaged food label.
  2. Review available label data from Open Food Facts when available.
  3. Check nutrient wins and watchouts for protein, fiber, sodium, sugar, saturated fat, and available vitamins and minerals.
  4. Save product scans locally on your device so you can compare products later.

Frequently asked questions

What does Deficify scan?

Deficify scans UPC and EAN barcodes on packaged foods, then shows available label nutrition, nutrient wins, watchouts, and source quality notes.

Does Deficify offer optional Plus features?

Yes. Deficify Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription at $3.99/month or $24.99/year, with a 7-day trial on annual for eligible users. The App Store listing shows the current app download price.

Does Deficify give medical advice?

No. Deficify provides educational label summaries only. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace clinician judgment.

Where does barcode nutrition data come from?

Deficify uses available label data from Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts data is available under ODbL and product photos under CC BY-SA. Food databases can be incomplete or out of date, so always check the package label.

Can I use Deficify without camera access?

Yes. Camera access is only for barcode scanning. You can type barcode digits manually if you do not grant camera permission.

Are saved scans private?

Saved scans are stored locally on your device. Deficify does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not sell personal data.

Deficify provides educational label summaries only. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace clinician judgment. Food databases can be incomplete or out of date, so always check the package label and talk with a qualified professional for medical or nutrition decisions.
